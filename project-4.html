Custom Actuator Design

Prototyping Mechanical Design
2023

Overview

Designed and prototyped a high-torque, compact actuator system for space-constrained robotic applications. The challenge was to achieve high torque output while maintaining a small form factor and low cost.

Through careful gear design and material selection, the actuator achieved the required torque specifications while reducing unit cost by $50 compared to off-the-shelf alternatives. The design has been used in multiple robotics projects.

Key Achievements

  • $50 cost reduction per unit
  • 15 Nm torque output in compact package
  • Modular design for easy integration
  • Validated through 1000+ hour testing

Design Process

1

Requirements & Analysis

Defined specifications: 15 Nm torque, 45mm max diameter, cost under $100. Analyzed existing solutions to identify improvement opportunities.

2

Gear Design

Designed custom planetary gear system for high torque density. Optimized gear ratios and tooth profiles for efficiency and manufacturability.

3

Prototyping

Built multiple iterations using 3D printing and CNC machining. Tested torque output, efficiency, and durability under various load conditions.

4

Optimization

Refined design based on test results. Reduced part count and simplified assembly process to lower manufacturing cost while maintaining performance.

Design Iterations & Tradeoffs

Iteration 1: Standard Gearbox

What Changed: Used off-the-shelf gearbox components

Why: Quick prototype to validate concept

Result: Met torque requirements but too expensive ($125/unit)

Iteration 2: Custom Design

What Changed: Designed custom planetary gear system with optimized geometry

Why: Need to reduce cost while maintaining performance

Tradeoff: Longer design time, required custom tooling

Result: $50 cost reduction ($75/unit), same performance, validated through testing
