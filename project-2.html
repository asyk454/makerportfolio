FTC Competition Robot

Robotics Competition
2023

Overview

Designed and built a competition robot for FIRST Tech Challenge, focusing on a custom drivetrain and manipulator systems. The robot needed to be fast, reliable, and capable of multiple game tasks including object manipulation and scoring.

This project was completed as part of a team, with me leading the mechanical design and drivetrain development. The final robot achieved 2x faster movement speed compared to our previous season's design while maintaining reliability throughout the competition.

Key Achievements

  • 2x faster movement speed vs. previous iteration
  • Top 8 finish at regional competition
  • Custom 6-wheel drivetrain with mecanum wheels
  • Dual manipulator system for game objects

Design Process

1

Game Analysis

Analyzed competition game rules and scoring opportunities. Identified key requirements: fast movement, object manipulation, and autonomous capabilities.

2

Drivetrain Design

Designed custom 6-wheel mecanum drivetrain for omnidirectional movement. Optimized wheel placement and gear ratios for maximum speed and maneuverability.

3

Manipulator Systems

Developed dual manipulator system: one for large objects, one for precision tasks. Used four-bar linkages and custom grippers optimized for game objects.

4

Prototyping & Testing

Built multiple iterations, testing speed, reliability, and game performance. Refined designs based on competition practice matches.

Design Iterations & Tradeoffs

Iteration 1: Standard Drivetrain

What Changed: Traditional 4-wheel tank drive

Why: Simple and reliable baseline

Result: Functional but slow, limited maneuverability

Iteration 2: Mecanum Upgrade

What Changed: Upgraded to 6-wheel mecanum drivetrain

Why: Need for omnidirectional movement and faster gameplay

Tradeoff: Increased complexity and cost

Result: 2x faster movement, better positioning, top 8 finish
